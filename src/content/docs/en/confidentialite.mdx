This site is a personal field notebook. It sells nothing, shows no ads and asks for no account.

## Audience measurement (Google Analytics)

The site measures its traffic with Google Analytics 4. Without your name or your email, it records:

- the pages viewed, and the page you came from (another site, a search engine, a QR code on the model page);
- the type of device, the operating system, the browser, the language, and the approximate country and region;
- two actions on the 3D model page: downloading the files (Google Earth, GLB) and launching augmented reality (on a
  table or at full scale).

**Why:** to know which pages are useful, whether the downloads and augmented reality get used, and where visitors come
from, to decide where the project goes next.

**Cookies:** Google Analytics sets cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_…`) to recognize a visit that carries on.
The data is processed by Google, outside Québec (in the United States), under its own rules. It is kept for
14 months, then deleted by Google.

**Opting out:** this button stops the measurement in this browser and deletes the Google Analytics cookies. It also lets
you accept it again.

## What stays in your browser

The 3D viewer keeps your settings (lighting, colours, etc.), and the site keeps your opt-out if you made one,
in your browser's local storage. This information doesn't leave your device and isn't a cookie.

## 3D viewer technical log

The 3D viewer sends the site a short technical log of each visit, to find and fix what doesn't work on some devices
(for example an iPhone running out of memory). It contains:

- the device as the browser describes it: browser and system, screen size, graphics card, memory;
- the model's loading time, the memory used, the length of the visit;
- the errors encountered, and the use of the augmented reality button;
- the country, inferred by the host.

It holds neither your name, nor your IP address, nor a cookie. Each visit gets a random code (shown at the bottom of
the viewer's ⚙ settings), which can't recognise you from one visit to the next. To spot a visit that ended badly, the
viewer keeps that code in the tab's storage for the length of the visit. The log is kept a few days at Cloudflare,
then deleted. It is used for nothing else.

## Hosting

The site, the 3D model and the downloads are hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web server, it
receives your IP address to send you the pages; this site has no access to it.
