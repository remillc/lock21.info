Lock 21 has been underwater since 1958. It is at Dickinson's Landing, one of the Lost Villages of the St. Lawrence, flooded when the Seaway opened. The tops of its walls lie 35 feet (10.7 m) below the surface and its floor at 60 feet (18.3 m). I set out to digitize the whole structure in 3D, zone by zone, dive after dive, with photogrammetry.

This site is the site of the project to digitize Lock 21, and the field notebook of that survey: what I did, how I did it, and what came out of it.

## What it is for

**Showing the lock to people who don't dive.** Few people will ever see it with their own eyes: it takes a dive to 60 feet, in a canal with a strong current. The [3D model](/en/modele) can be explored in the browser, on a computer or a phone. It can also be placed on a table in augmented reality, or opened in Google Earth at its real location.

**Helping divers plan their dives.** The [site zones](/en/decoupage-site) page describes the lock's 25 zones, and the page on [the current in the canal](/en/problemes/fort-courant-canal) tells divers what to expect. The 3D model lets you find your way around and check depths before getting in the water.

**Documenting the method.** Underwater photogrammetry is learned by getting it wrong. I write everything down so that other divers can do it elsewhere:

- the [gear and its settings](/en/collecte/materiel-utilise);
- the [dive log](/en/collecte/journal-plongees);
- the processing, from [extracting the photos](/en/traitement/extraction-photos) to the [model](/en/traitement/generation-modeles);
- the problems encountered and the solutions found (see the menu).

## Where the survey stands

The survey moves forward one zone at a time: the [3D model page](/en/modele) shows how many zones are online, and the [progress tracking](/en/traitement/suivi) gives the state of each one. The site grows with the model.

## Install the app

<InstallApp />

The site installs as an app, on a phone, a tablet or a computer. It then opens from the home screen, without the browser's bars, and the pages already read stay available without a network: handy by the water, where the signal is weak. The 3D model still needs a connection.
