Does ambient light affect the colour chart?

- If so, how many depth levels should get their own correction?

## Tests carried out

- Film the colour chart at the lowest point of the lock (floor behind pillar 3)
- Film the colour chart at the highest point of the lock (on top of pillar 3)

## In detail

1. Pick a reference distance between the camera and the chart (about 60cm) and keep that distance for every shot.
2. For each of the 2 shots below, film for about 3 seconds and show with the fingers of one hand how many lamps are on at the start of the sequence

### On the floor behind pillar 3

1. Note the depth of the colour chart:
2. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = 0` (default value)
3. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = -1`
4. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = -2`

### On top of pillar 3

1. Note the depth at the top of pillar 3:
2. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = 0` (default value)
3. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = -1`
4. Shot of the colour chart with `Ev comp = -2`
