So that each model has its real size and the zones line up with each other (see
[Aligning chunks](/en/problemes/alignement-chunks)), I measure distances on the site during the dives: two targets
placed on the bottom, the distance between them measured, and both targets filmed with the rest of the zone. The
distance between two targets makes a scale.

## The targets

Targets of 7.5 cm and 15 cm, numbered in pairs (targets 1 and 2).

Their pattern looks abstract, but each one carries a coded number. They are Metashape's circular coded targets: the
black dot in the centre marks the exact point to measure, and the black sectors of the ring around it make the code,
each position on the ring a binary digit, black or white. Metashape reads that ring in the photos, recognises each
target's number and finds the same target from one photo to the next.
