---
title: Colour calibration
description: "The colour chart filmed on both sides at two depths, to see whether the ambient light changes with depth and how to make up for it."
---
Videos of both sides of the colour chart, shot at 2 different depths to find out whether the influence of ambient light changes with depth. The different exposures were meant to find a compensation factor for the camera overexposing the chart. -1 stop is the right factor.
