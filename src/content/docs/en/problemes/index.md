The problems met along the way, from filming underwater to the 3D model, and the tests I ran to solve them. Some pages are still working notes.

## While filming

- [The colour chart is overexposed](/en/problemes/charte-couleurs-surexposee): at 60 cm the chart fills only a small corner of the frame, and the bright floor around it throws off the exposure. Conclusion: shoot it one stop under.
- [Colour calibration](/en/problemes/calibration-couleurs): the chart filmed on both sides at two depths, to see whether the ambient light changes with depth and how to compensate for it.
- [Image sharpness](/en/problemes/nettete-images): the first images, taken while moving, were blurry, and the point cloud lacked precision. The camera, the water, the lighting and the settings (swimming speed, shutter speed, ISO) all play a part.
- [The strong current in the canal](/en/problemes/fort-courant-canal): how the scooter behaves with the rig (the camera frame) and camera in the current, and how fast the water moves: 35 feet a minute (10.7 m/min), measured by drifting down the canal on June 8, 2024.
- [Camera rig dimensions](/en/problemes/dimensions-rig): the GoPro's field of view measured on the floor at 70 cm, and its lens's angles of view.

## In processing

- [Banana effect](/en/problemes/effet-banane): a flat 100-foot floor came out curved like a banana after alignment in Metashape. The likely cause: the GoPro's wide-angle lens distortion. The leads: calibrate the camera, crop the images' edges, a less wide lens.
- [Aligning chunks](/en/problemes/alignement-chunks): each zone's model comes out slightly distorted, which makes them hard to line up with each other. Targets give each one its real size; the distortion isn't fully solved yet.
