---
title: Aligning chunks
description: Why the zones' models don't line up well with each other, and how targets give them their real size.
---

Each zone is processed separately in Metashape, in its own *chunk* (a set of photos processed on its own), and every
model comes out of the process slightly distorted. These distortions make it hard to line the models up with each
other.

The size has to be right too: a model that isn't exactly to scale won't line up with its neighbours. So I used targets
to set measuring scales on each model and bring it to its exact real dimensions.
